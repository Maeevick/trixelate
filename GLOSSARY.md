# Glossary

The words trixelate's code and docs use, read before naming anything.

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Picture** | The image a sheet is made from, opaque: its transparent parts read as white paper. |
| **Grid** | The columns and rows of cells a picture is simplified into. |
| **Cell** | One square the child colours in: a pixel for the children, not for the screen. |
| **Palette** | The dominant colours of a grid, as many as asked or fewer, the most used first. |
| **Colour number** | A colour's place in the palette, from 1, written in every cell to paint with it. |
| **Colouring** | A grid's cells, each with its colour number, and the palette they are numbered from. |
| **Sheet** | The page printed for the child: the grid of numbered cells and its legend. |
| **Page format** | The paper the sheet is drawn for, A3, A4 or A5 upright, at 300 dots per inch. |
| **Margin** | The blank paper around the sheet's content, 20 mm unless asked otherwise. |
| **Legend** | The row of bubbles at the bottom of a sheet. |
| **Bubble** | A disc filled with one palette colour, its colour number written inside. |
