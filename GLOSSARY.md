# Glossary

The words trixelate's code and docs use, read before naming anything.

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Picture** | The image a sheet is made from, opaque: its transparent parts read as white paper. |
| **Grid** | The columns and rows of cells a picture is simplified into, as many columns as asked and the rows that keep the picture's proportions. |
| **Cell** | One square the child colours in: a pixel for the children, not for the screen. |
| **Palette** | The dominant colours of a grid, as many as asked or fewer, the most used first. |
| **Colour number** | A colour's place in the palette, from 1, written in every cell to paint with it. |
| **Colouring** | A grid's cells, each with its colour number, and the palette they are numbered from. |
| **Sheet** | The page printed for the child: the grid of numbered cells and its legend. |
| **Model** | The picture as the child will have coloured it: a block of colour per cell, with no number. Every sheet shows it in miniature, and its preview at full size. |
| **Miniature** | The model drawn small in the page's top left corner of every sheet, framed, with no number and no line, for the child to see what they are making. It is seven thirty-seconds of the grid wide, over the grid where they meet. |
| **Preview** | A sheet as it looks once coloured in: every cell filled with its colour inside its outline, over its number, the rest of the sheet unchanged. |
| **Page format** | The paper the sheet is drawn for, from A2 to A5, every one laid out at 300 dots per inch and written in shapes. |
| **Orientation** | Which way up the paper lies: portrait, upright, or landscape, on its side. Unless told otherwise, the way the picture suits: on its side once it is a tenth wider than tall. |
| **Margin** | The blank paper around the sheet's content, 10 mm unless asked otherwise, from 5 mm, the room the title and the credit need, to 20 mm, leaving the room to the cells. |
| **Legend** | The row of bubbles of a sheet: upright, right of the miniature and centred on its middle; on its side, centred at the bottom. |
| **Credit** | The small line at the bottom right of every sheet, in its margin: made with trixelate, and where it lives. |
| **Title** | The sheet's file name without its stamp, centred in the top margin of every sheet, as big as the bubbles' numbers. |
| **Stamp** | The UTC second a sheet was made, written into the name of a file trixelate names itself, before what it is: `goblin-32-4-a4-20261111T142915Z.sheet.pdf`. It keeps a sheet made again from taking an older one's name, numbered `-2`, `-3` after it when sheets made that second took the name, and never shows on the paper. |
| **Bubble** | A disc filled with one palette colour, its colour number written inside, up to 16 mm wide on every paper. |
| **Piece** | One A4 page of an A2 or A3 sheet cut for a home printer: its part of the sheet inside a 10 mm border cut away along fine dashed lines, its row and column written under it, butted edge to edge with the next. A sheet takes the A4 pages its paper is made of, 4 for an A2 and 2 for an A3, shrunk to 90% to fit, and they come as one PDF, row after row. |
